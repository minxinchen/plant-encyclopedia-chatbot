## External evidence

- Internet Archive and Biodiversity Heritage Library material is used only to
  align pages, OCR and entry boundaries for the same historical edition.
- Global Names GNfinder/GNverifier output is used only to detect and validate
  scientific-name candidates at entry boundaries.
- TaiCOL, Tai2 and other Taiwan public sources are used only for traceable name
  metadata. They do not supply or alter facts attributed to the book.
- Model weights and API responses are not included in the source-code license.

Every derived artifact must retain its source locator and provenance hashes.
External evidence must never be promoted as a statement made by the book.
